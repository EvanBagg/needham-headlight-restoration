The restoration

Four steps. Done in your driveway.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We check lens condition and mask surrounding trim and paint before any work starts.

  2. 02

    Clean oxidation

    The clouded, UV-damaged outer layer is stripped away to reveal clear polycarbonate underneath.

  3. 03

    Buff scratches

    Progressive polishing compounds remove fine scratches and haze for an optically clear finish.

  4. 04

    Seal with clear coat

    A UV-resistant clear coat film locks in the clarity so results last, not fade back in months.

About us

Two guys, a polisher, and a lot of foggy headlights.

We're a small, local headlight restoration team based in Needham, MA. We started this because too many cars around town had headlights that had gone cloudy, yellow, or hazy — hurting both the way the car looks and how well its lights actually work at night.

Every job follows the same guaranteed 4-step process, we're fully mobile, and we work around your schedule — evenings and weekends included.

The restoration

Four steps. Done in your driveway.

    Assess

    We check lens condition and mask surrounding trim and paint before any work starts.

    Clean oxidation

About us

WHO WE ARE

Needham Headlight Restoration was started by Evan and Neil, two friends from Needham who noticed a simple problem: cloudy, yellow, and oxidized headlights are everywhere, but getting them professionally restored can be expensive and inconvenient.

The idea started in summer 2025 when Neil restored his own headlights with a kit. After seeing the results, we looked into local options and found that some mechanic shops charged as much as $125 for headlight restoration, often requiring customers to leave their car at the shop. We realized we could offer a more convenient and affordable solution by bringing the service directly to the customer.

We’re a mobile service based in Needham, traveling to surrounding towns to restore your headlights at your convenience. Today, we’re both college students — Evan studies business at Fairfield University and Neil studies mechanical engineering at WPI — while continuing to build the local business we started together.
